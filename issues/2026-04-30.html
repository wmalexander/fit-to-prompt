SoftBank Spins Up a Robot Data-Center Builder, Eyes a $100B IPO

TechCrunch · Apr 29

SoftBank is launching Roze AI, a new venture that uses autonomous robots to automate the construction of data centers across the United States. The company is already aiming at a 2026 IPO with a potential $100 billion valuation, before a single facility has shipped.

The pitch is the entire AI capex story compressed into one company: the bottleneck on AI is concrete and steel, not GPUs, and whoever automates the build wins twice, once in margin and once in pace.
