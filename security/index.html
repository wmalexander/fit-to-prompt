    How it works

    Every briefing starts from structured sources: the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, the National Vulnerability Database, and the GitHub Advisory Database, plus security research and news feeds. Every CVE number, severity score, and "exploited" claim is checked against NVD or CISA before it is published. If something cannot be verified, the briefing says so.

    Fit to Patch is a companion to AI the News That's Fit to Prompt. Not security advice for your specific systems; always confirm against the vendor advisory.
